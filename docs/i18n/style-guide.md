# Translation Style Guide

> **Default personality: Casually silly, friendly, and approachable.**
>
> Translations should preserve PoxBot's personality while prioritizing accuracy, clarity, and natural language.

## 1. General Principles

All translations should follow these principles:

- **Meaning over literal translation:** Preserve the original intent rather than translating word for word.
- **Natural language:** Use expressions that native speakers would naturally use.
- **Consistent personality:** Keep PoxBot friendly, casual, playful, and approachable.
- **Context awareness:** Adapt the tone to the situation and audience.
- **Technical accuracy:** Never sacrifice correctness for humor or personality.
- **Cultural adaptation:** Adapt jokes, idioms, and expressions when direct translations do not work.
- **Conciseness:** Prefer clear and reasonably short messages, especially in Discord interactions.

## 2. Voice and Personality

PoxBot should generally sound:

- Casual rather than unnecessarily formal.
- Friendly rather than distant.
- Playful rather than robotic.
- Expressive rather than monotonous.
- Approachable rather than overly professional.

Lighthearted humor, wordplay, and silly expressions are encouraged when appropriate.

However, humor should never make important information confusing or misleading.

## 3. Tone Guidelines

Choose the tone according to the context.

| Context | Recommended tone |
|---|---|
| General interactions | Casual and playful |
| Greetings and welcome messages | Friendly and enthusiastic |
| Successful operations | Cheerful and expressive |
| Errors and failures | Friendly, reassuring, and helpful |
| Warnings | Clear and appropriately serious |
| Moderation | Neutral, respectful, and professional |
| Privacy and security | Professional, precise, and unambiguous |
| Legal information | Formal and accurate |
| Administrative operations | Professional but approachable |
| System notifications | Concise and informative |

### Formality Rules

- Use casual language by default.
- Use polite or formal language when the situation requires it.
- Serious situations must take priority over PoxBot's playful personality.
- Do not force jokes into warnings, security messages, moderation decisions, or legal information.
- Avoid unnecessarily formal wording in ordinary interactions.

## 4. Language and Grammar

- Follow the target language's grammatical conventions.
- Prefer idiomatic expressions over literal translations.
- Respect language-specific punctuation and typography.
- Avoid unnatural sentence structures copied from English.
- Use consistent terminology throughout the translation.
- Do not introduce unnecessary slang or regional expressions.
- Avoid excessive honorifics or overly complicated wording unless appropriate.

## 5. Formatting and Placeholders

Preserve all special formatting and dynamic placeholders.

Examples:

- `{user}`
- `{user.mention}`
- `{server.name}`
- `{current}`
- `{total}`

Additional requirements:

- Do not translate placeholder names.
- Preserve Discord-specific formatting and Markdown.
- Preserve URLs, identifiers, and technical values.
- Do not accidentally remove line breaks or formatting.
- Respect any documented character limits.

## 6. Humor and Expressions

Humor is encouraged, but it should be appropriate to the context.

Recommended:
- Lighthearted jokes.
- Natural wordplay.
- Friendly expressions.
- Creative but understandable wording.
- Language-specific humor that preserves the original intention.

Avoid:
- Forced memes and outdated internet slang.
- Humor that could be interpreted as offensive.
- Excessive emoticons or emoji.
- Jokes in situations requiring serious communication.
- Expressions that make important instructions ambiguous.

## 7. Consistency

Translators should:

- Reuse established terminology.
- Follow existing translations where appropriate.
- Avoid translating the same technical term inconsistently.
- Maintain consistent naming conventions.
- Refer to the language-specific style guide when available.

When an established translation is unsuitable for a particular context, prioritize clarity and document the reasoning.

## 8. Technical Terminology

Keep technical terminology accurate and recognizable.

Examples include:
- Discord commands and interactions.
- Permissions and moderation terminology.
- Database and configuration terminology.
- Authentication and security terminology.
- Programming and development terminology.

Use commonly accepted terminology in the target language. Retain the original technical term when translating it would make it less recognizable.

## 9. Language-Specific Conventions

Each language may have additional conventions.

These can include:
- Formality and politeness levels.
- Preferred terminology.
- Punctuation and typography.
- Date, time, and number formats.
- Regional vocabulary.
- Language-specific humor and expressions.

See the corresponding language-specific guide for additional requirements.

## 10. Translation Review Checklist

Before submitting translations, verify:

- [ ] The original meaning is preserved.
- [ ] The translation sounds natural to native speakers.
- [ ] The tone matches the situation.
- [ ] PoxBot's personality is preserved where appropriate.
- [ ] Serious information remains clear and professional.
- [ ] All placeholders are preserved.
- [ ] Markdown and Discord formatting are intact.
- [ ] Terminology is consistent.
- [ ] No unnecessary information has been introduced.
- [ ] The translation follows the target language's conventions.

---

**Final principle:**

PoxBot should feel like the same friendly, casually silly bot in every language, not a literal translation of an English chatbot.

Be playful when appropriate, professional when necessary, and accurate at all times.
